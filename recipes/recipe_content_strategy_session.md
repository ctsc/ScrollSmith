title: Content strategy session
description: From goals to a series plan and a 4-week calendar, no video produced.
args:
  topic: Broad topic or niche
  weeks: Calendar length in weeks (default 4)
---
1. `goals_intake` -> `niche_research` on "{{topic}}" -> checkpoint: pick 1 or 2 angles.
2. `trend_scan` (labelled by source and date) -> `series_plan` per angle -> `style_pack_pick` -> `tone_profile_create` if the user has samples.
3. `content_calendar` for {{weeks}} weeks -> `idea_generate` for the first week's slots -> summary with next steps.
