# Long-form (YouTube 16:9) rulebook

Format: 8 to 20 minute videos. House rules.

## workflow
footage_ingest -> edit_plan_create (long_form defaults) -> b-roll plan -> caption sidecar -> audio_mix -> render_preview (first 2 minutes) -> qa_check -> render_final -> cover_frame 1280x720.

## structure
- Cold open up to 20 s, intro card at most 1.5 s, chapter title at each topic change, retention reset (visual change or b-roll block) at least every 90 s.
- Payoff promised in the open and delivered after the midpoint.

## pacing
- Remove dead air over 600 ms and fillers; keep natural breathing. Visual change at least every 5 s.

## captions
- Sidecar SRT and VTT by default; burn-in only for key quotes.

## audio
- -14 LUFS, music 18 to 22 dB under voice, drop music entirely during emotional peaks.

## export
- 1920x1080 H.264 CRF 18 or 2160p when the source allows; chapters in the metadata with timestamps.
