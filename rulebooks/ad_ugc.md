# Ad and UGC rulebook

Format: product or offer videos in a creator style. House rules; claims and disclosures are the user's legal responsibility.

## workflow
goals_intake -> script_write (beat map hook, problem, demo, proof, offer, cta) -> fact_check on every claim -> footage_ingest or scene_plan_create -> edit_plan_create -> caption_apply_style -> audio_mix -> render_preview -> qa_check -> policy_check -> render_final -> export_package.

## structure
- Hook under 3 s with the product visible or named. Problem in one line. Demo is the longest beat. Proof (review, number, before and after) must be verifiable. Offer and CTA in the last quarter.
- 15 to 35 s. Visual change every 2 s or faster.

## claims
- Every factual or comparative claim goes through `fact_check`; unverified claims are removed or softened. No health, earnings or guaranteed results claims.
- Paid partnership disclosure in the metadata and on screen when required by the platform.

## captions
- Always on (muted feeds). Preset `pop_in_3word` or brand preset. Price and offer text in a safe-zone slot, not in captions.

## audio
- Music above 120 BPM for energetic products, ducked under voice. Prefer tracks cleared for commercial use; Content ID risk matters less for ads but license does.

## export
- Variant per platform; Meta ads safe zone (`meta_ads`) for Reels placements. CTA end card 1.5 s.
